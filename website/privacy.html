Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

MedCheck is a free app run by a team of high school students. This page explains what information the MedCheck app and website collect, why, and how you can delete it. The short version: you can use MedCheck without an account, we don't show ads, and we never sell your information.

If you use MedCheck without an account

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal information.
  • Searches happen on your device. The claims are built into the app, so what you type in the search box isn't sent to us.
  • Claims you save are stored only on your device. Clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes them.

If you create an account

An account lets you keep your saved claims across devices. When you make one, we store:

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and reset your password.
  • Your password, stored in a scrambled (hashed) form by our account provider. Nobody on our team can see it.
  • The list of claims you've saved and when you saved them.
  • Basic account records, like when the account was created and when you last signed in.

We use this only to sign you in, sync your saved claims, and send emails about your account, like a link to confirm your email or reset your password. We don't send marketing emails.

Information our service providers receive

Like almost every app and website, MedCheck uses outside services to run:

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved claims.
  • Vercel hosts the app and this website.
  • Our email delivery provider sends account emails.
  • Google Fonts provides the app's fonts.

When your device connects to these services, they receive standard technical information such as your IP address and the type of device and browser you use. They use it to deliver the service and keep it secure. They don't receive your saved claims unless they store them for us (Supabase).

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads or share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't ask for health information about you. MedCheck is about claims, not your medical history.

Kids and teens

You must be 13 or older to create a MedCheck account. Anyone can use MedCheck without an account. If we learn that someone under 13 has made an account, we'll delete it. Parents who think their child under 13 made an account can email us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Account, then tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your email address and saved claims from our database right away. You can also follow the steps on our delete your account page, or email us.

Copies kept in our providers' routine security backups or logs may remain for a short time before they are automatically removed.

Keeping information safe

Connections to MedCheck are encrypted. Saved claims are protected by database rules that let each account read and change only its own saved claims. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. For big changes, we'll also let account holders know in the app or by email.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your information: team-email@example.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.
