Delete your MedCheck account

This page is for the MedCheck app by the MedCheck team, on iPhone, Android and the web.

Option 1: in the app or in any web browser

  1. Open the MedCheck app, or go to medcheck-steel.vercel.app/account in any browser.
  2. Sign in with the account you want to delete.
  3. Tap the person icon at the top right to open Account and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out.

Option 2: email us

If you can't sign in, email team-email@example.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my MedCheck account." We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and password.
  • Your saved claims list.
  • Your account records, such as when you signed up and last signed in.

What may be kept for a short time

Copies in our providers' routine security backups and server logs may remain for a short time before they are automatically removed. We don't use them to restore deleted accounts. Saved claims stored only on your device stay there until you clear your browser data or delete the app.

Want to delete only your saved claims, not your whole account? Open Saved and tap each claim's save button to remove it.
