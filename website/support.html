Help and contact

Email us any time at team-email@example.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Suggest a claim

Saw a health claim you want checked? Email it to us with a link to where you saw it, like a TikTok, post or article. We can't check every claim, but suggestions help us choose what to research next.

Report a mistake

If you think a rating or explanation is wrong, email us the claim's name and what you think is off. A link to a study helps a lot. We review every report and update the claim if the evidence supports it.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything in MedCheck works without an account. An account just keeps your saved claims across devices.

I forgot my password

Tap the person icon at the top right to open Account in the app, tap Forgot your password?, and enter your email. We'll send you a link to choose a new password. Check your spam folder if it doesn't show up in a few minutes.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or promotions folder. If it's not there after 10 minutes, try signing up again or email us.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account and tap Delete my account. See all the ways to delete your account.

How do you decide the ratings?

We look at the strongest research available, like systematic reviews and large trials, and rate how strong the evidence is on a four-level scale. The app's How we rate evidence page explains each level.

Is MedCheck medical advice?

No. MedCheck is for learning about research. For your own health, talk to a doctor, nurse or pharmacist. In an emergency, call 911.
